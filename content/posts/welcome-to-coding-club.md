Welcome to our official Coding Club portal!

Whether you are writing your first line of Python, building scalable cloud architectures, or exploring machine learning, our club is built by learners, for learners.

### What is the Coding Club?
We are an open community of student developers, enthusiasts, and creators focused on:
- **Weekly Hands-on Workshops**: From full-stack Next.js to system design and algorithm challenges.
- **Open Source Projects**: Building real tools used across our campus and beyond.
- **Hackathons & Competitions**: Team up, build bold prototypes, and solve problems in 24 hours.

### How Content Works on this Portal
This site is powered by **Decap CMS** running directly on **Next.js** and **Vercel**!
- Anyone with access can visit `/admin`.
- Write your blog post or add upcoming events in an intuitive rich-text editor.
- Hit **Publish**, and Decap automatically commits the markdown post directly to our GitHub repository.
- Vercel rebuilds in seconds and the article goes live automatically!

Stay tuned for our upcoming workshops!
